# Probe (scion)

The grade arrived as your wake message. Every correction and newly learned fact is a probe candidate: a grade that taught you anything leaves seams — an implication you now guess at, a correction whose reach you can't verify, the intent behind a commitment. Project your likely first tasks: anything you'd need the elder for is a probe question.

Compose your questions — if the seam-search truly yields nothing, say so. Then hand off: send them to the elder (the grade's `from`) as the message body (summary `quickening → run reply`), arm the watcher, end the turn.
